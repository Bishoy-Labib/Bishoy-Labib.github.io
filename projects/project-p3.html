Deep Drawing FEA

Project Overview

As part of the Non-Linear Finite Element Methods course, I simulated a deep drawing process using Explicit Dynamics to predict material behavior under high-strain conditions.

  • Used the ANSYS Explicit solver to model high-strain rate deformations during the deep drawing of metal blanks.
  • Developed material models using Bilinear Isotropic Hardening and Plastic Strain Failure criteria to define the blank’s post-yield behavior.
  • Applied frictionless contact and rigid body assumptions to the punch and die to streamline the computational solve.
  • Analyzed punch force and total equivalent plastic strain to identify potential fracture and blanking points.
  • Evaluated energy error and mass scaling to verify the stability and accuracy of the explicit dynamics solution.
