FEA ANSYS 2023–24

Manufacturing Validation & FEA

Project Overview

Applied advanced Finite Element Analysis techniques for manufacturing validation and structural compliance, covering deep drawing simulation, failure prediction, and large-scale structural verification per Eurocode 3.

Key Contributions

  • Used Explicit Dynamics solver in ANSYS to simulate high-strain rate deformations in deep drawing processes.
  • Identified fracture and blanking points before physical tooling was committed, saving significant tooling costs.
  • Developed high-fidelity material models with Bilinear Isotropic Hardening and Plastic Strain Failure criteria.
  • Verified structural compliance of large-scale steel structures per Eurocode 3 (EN 1993) under varying load conditions.
