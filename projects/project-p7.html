Optimization ofMedical Device Manufacturing Technology

Project Overview

Bachelor's thesis project focused on the full manufacturing process optimization of orthodontic pliers - a precision medical device used in dental and orthodontic practice. The work applied an integrated CAD/CAM/CAE methodology to redesign and optimize the manufacturing process while ensuring compliance with EU MDR and ISO 13485 quality standards. This project was awarded 3rd Place at the Scientific Students' Associations Conference (TDK).

Key Work

  • 3D modelling and design optimization of orthodontic plier fixtures
  • CAM toolpath generation and CNC machining simulation for precision manufacturing.
  • CAE structural and stress analysis (FEA) to validate durability and performance.
  • Compliance documentation to EU MDR and ISO 13485 medical device regulations.
  • Manufacturing process documentation and quality control plan development.
