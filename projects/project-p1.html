ROS2 Digital Twin 2024

Digital Twin & Robotics Integration

Project Overview

Developed a fully synchronized Digital Twin pipeline for the SO-100 robot, bridging Fusion 360 CAD designs with ROS2-based motion planning environments. The project targets Industry 5.0 human-robot collaboration paradigms.

Key Contributions

  • Developed a synchronized Digital Twin pipeline for the SO-100 robot, enabling seamless CAD-to-simulation transfer.
  • Reduced hardware risk by validating complex kinematic movements in Unity and NVIDIA Isaac Sim before physical deployment.
  • Ensured virtual and physical environments were fully aligned through coordinate frame calibration and sensor fusion.
  • Standardized the deployment workflow to accelerate integration of simulation tools for research and educational applications.

Technologies Used

ROS2, URDF/XACRO, Fusion 360, Unity, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Python, Sim2Real, HIL, Git.
