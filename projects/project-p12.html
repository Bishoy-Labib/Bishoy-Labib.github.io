FEM ANSYS 2022 - 2025

FEM & Simulation Studies

Project Overview

A curated collection of Finite Element Method assignments and analysis projects completed across my engineering studies. The work spans structural, thermal, dynamic, and nonlinear simulations using ANSYS Mechanical, covering real-world engineering problems from stress analysis to vibration and buckling studies.

Topics Covered

  • Static structural analysis - stress, strain, and deformation under load.
  • Modal and harmonic analysis - natural frequencies and forced vibration response.
  • Nonlinear FEA - large deformation, contact mechanics, and plasticity.
  • Thermal analysis - heat transfer, temperature distribution, and thermal stress coupling.
  • Buckling analysis - critical load prediction for slender structures.
  • Mesh convergence and singularity studies and element selection validation.
