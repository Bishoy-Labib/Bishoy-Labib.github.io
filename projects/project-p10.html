Metrology Precision 2024–25

Tool Presetter Retrofit

Project Overview

Retrofit and restoration of a tool presetter machine used for precision tool length and diameter measurement in CNC machining operations. The project involved a full mechanical overhaul, alignment calibration, and evaluation of measurement system capability to restore the machine to working precision metrology standards.

Key Work

  • Disassembly and inspection of all mechanical components — axes, optics, and fixtures.
  • Cleaning, lubrication, and replacement of worn or damaged parts.
  • Geometric alignment and calibration of the measurement system.
  • Measurement system analysis (MSA / Gauge R&R) to validate restored accuracy.
  • Documentation of the retrofit process and new calibration intervals.
