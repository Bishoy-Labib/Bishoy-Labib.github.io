Automotive Quality Engineering

Project Overview

Led quality engineering activities for automotive (IATF 16949) and injection molding (ISO 20457) supply chains at ZALNER Engineering, focusing on data-driven quality systems and supplier qualification.

Key Contributions

  • Applied ISO 9001, ISO GPS, and ASME Y14.5 GD&T standards across automotive and injection molding workflows.
  • Developed data-driven QA using PPAP, SPC, 4D, and 8D reports with root cause analysis.
  • Managed acceptance and release of inspection processes and purchased parts via APQP methodologies.
  • Coordinated with international clients on technical requirements and non-conformance resolution.
