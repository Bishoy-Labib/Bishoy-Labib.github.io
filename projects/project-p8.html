Electronics Embedded 2023–24

Stepper Motor Controller

Project Overview

Design and implementation of a stepper motor controller, covering electronics hardware design, PCB layout, firmware development, and mechanical integration. The project demonstrates a full-stack embedded systems approach — from schematic design to physical build and testing.

Key Work

  • Schematic design and PCB layout for the motor driver circuit.
  • Firmware development for motion control and step/direction signalling.
  • Integration with mechanical assemblies for position-controlled actuation.
  • Testing and validation of speed, torque, and positional accuracy.
