Materials Testing 2022 - 2024

Material Characterisation

Project Overview

Practical material characterisation work covering the mechanical testing, microstructural analysis, and property evaluation of a range of engineering materials. This work was carried out across laboratory sessions and research activities, building hands-on experience with standard characterisation methods used in industry and academia.

Key Work

  • Tensile, compression, hardness, and charpy testing of metallic and polymeric specimens.
  • Microstructural analysis using optical and electron microscopy techniques.
  • Heat treatment experiments (annealing, quenching, tempering) and property evaluation.
  • Fracture surface analysis and failure mode classification.
  • Interpretation and reporting of experimental results against material datasheets and standards.
